Health Scoring Rules

Understand how the system calculates each container's score.

SCORING METHODOLOGY

Risk Classification Criteria

  • Each running container starts with a score of 100.
  • A container that is not running receives a score of 0.
  • CPU usage at 60%, 75% and 90% triggers increasing penalties.
  • Memory usage at 60%, 75% and 90% triggers increasing penalties.
  • Three or more restarts trigger a penalty; five or more trigger a larger penalty.
  • Scores of 80–100 are Healthy, 50–79 are Warning, and below 50 are Critical.

These are configurable project rules, not guaranteed predictions of failure. Review the findings alongside actual container behavior.
